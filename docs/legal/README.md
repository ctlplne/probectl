# Legal readiness packet

These files are engineering-prepared inputs for licensed counsel. They are
**not legal advice, not approved contract language, and not permission to use a
third party's data or marks**.

The packet deliberately separates three things:

1. **Founder decisions** — product facts counsel should encode, not reopen by
   accident.
2. **Recommended starting terms** — a concrete first draft that saves counsel
   discovery time, but which counsel may change.
3. **Open legal decisions** — questions that require the actual legal entity,
   jurisdictions, deal posture, or a rights holder's written permission.

Start with
[`counsel-readiness-2026-08-09.md`](counsel-readiness-2026-08-09.md), then use
[`open-data-source-review-2026-08-09.md`](open-data-source-review-2026-08-09.md)
for the external-data schedule. The machine-readable mirror is
[`counsel-checklist.json`](counsel-checklist.json).

Do not describe `ee/LICENSE` as final. The repository root `LICENSE` is the
final text for first-party source outside `ee/`: the unmodified BUSL-1.1 with
its parameters for the core, then the unmodified MPL-2.0 for `pkg/`, `proto/`
and `examples/`; the bespoke commercial paper is still counsel-owned.
